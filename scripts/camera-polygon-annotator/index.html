Tips:
• Single click adds a point.
• Finish needs at least 3 points.
• Use multiple include polygons for separate holding lanes.
• Use exclusion polygons for intersections, driveways, crosswalks, or any break that should split/ignore queue detection.
• To edit: click a polygon in the image or click Edit in the list, then drag points, click an edge to add a point, or update id/type/label above.
• Exclude parked cars, opposing traffic, shoulders, side streets, sidewalks, and through lanes.
